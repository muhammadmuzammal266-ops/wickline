Community rules

Last updated 15 October 2026

Ideas and comments on RealChart are public. Anyone can read them, with the name and photo of the person who posted them. These rules keep the Ideas page useful and safe for everyone.

The rules

  1. Share your own analysis. Say what you see on the chart and why. If you build on someone else's work, say so.
  2. No spam or selling. No adverts, referral links, paid groups, signal services or repeated posts.
  3. Be respectful. Disagree with the idea, not the person. No abuse, harassment, hate or threats.
  4. No scams or deliberately misleading posts. No "guaranteed profit" claims, pump-and-dump calls or pretending to be someone else.
  5. Nothing illegal, sexual or violent.
  6. Keep personal details private. Don't post anyone's address, phone number, account details or other personal information, including your own.
  7. Remember it's an opinion. Ideas aren't financial advice. A good idea says what would prove it wrong.

What happens if something breaks the rules

  • Anyone signed in can press Report on an idea or a comment. Reports go only to the site's moderator.
  • The moderator may hide or delete ideas and comments that break these rules, and may remove accounts that keep breaking them.
  • You can delete your own ideas and comments at any time, and comments posted on your own ideas.
  • Once an idea is published, its chart and its long or short call can't be changed, so ideas can be judged fairly later. You can fix the title and text for an hour after publishing. After that, add a comment to give an update.

Limits

To keep spam away, each person can publish up to 10 ideas a day and post up to 20 comments in 10 minutes.

Questions

To ask about these rules or a moderation decision, open an issue on the site's GitHub page. See also the privacy policy.
