What is RealChart? A free TradingView alternative

A fast, modern charting platform for crypto, stocks, forex, indices and commodities — with the tools serious chartists expect, and nothing to pay.

Published 11 November 2026 · RealChart

RealChart is a free, browser-based charting platform in the style of TradingView. It gives you live, interactive price charts for thousands of markets — cryptocurrencies, US stocks, forex pairs, stock indices and commodities — together with the indicators, drawing tools and alerts you need to analyse them. There's no account to create and no subscription: you open the site and start charting.

What you can do with it

  • Live charts across crypto, stocks, forex, indices and commodities, with multiple timeframes from one minute to one month.
  • 100+ technical indicators — moving averages, RSI, MACD, Bollinger Bands, VWAP and many more — all included, none paywalled.
  • Drawing tools for trendlines, support and resistance, Fibonacci levels, channels and annotations.
  • Watchlists to follow the markets you care about at a glance.
  • Price alerts that reach your phone, so you're notified when a market crosses your level — even when the site is closed.
  • A paper-trading journal to practise and track ideas without risking real money.

How it compares to TradingView

TradingView is excellent, but many of its best features — extra indicators per chart, more alerts, faster data and ad-free use — sit behind a monthly subscription. RealChart's aim is simpler: give individual traders a clean, capable charting tool where the core features are free for everyone. You won't hit a paywall when you add a fifth indicator or try to set one more alert.

Who it's for

RealChart is built for anyone who wants to look at markets properly without paying for a terminal: crypto traders watching coins around the clock, stock investors checking charts before a decision, forex traders following the majors, and complete beginners learning how price action works. Because it runs in the browser, there's nothing to install — it works on a laptop or a phone.

Is it really free?

Yes. The charts, indicators, drawing tools, watchlists and alerts are free to use with no sign-up. We explain how the site supports itself in Why RealChart is free.
