How to set price alerts that reach your phone

You can't watch the market all day — so let it tell you when something happens. Here's how to set a free alert that reaches your phone.

Published 11 November 2026 · RealChart

A price alert watches a market for you and notifies you the moment it crosses a level you set — a breakout, a support test, a target. On RealChart, alerts are free, and they can reach your phone as a push notification even when the site is closed. Here's how to set one up.

Step 1 — Open the market

Open the chart for whatever you want to watch — a cryptocurrency, a stock, a forex pair, an index or a commodity. Use the search to jump straight to it.

Step 2 — Create the alert

There are two quick ways:

  • Type your target into the "Alert me when … crosses" box in the side panel and press Add.
  • Or pick up the bell / alert tool on the chart and click the price level you want — handy for setting an alert right on a support or resistance line.

You can choose whether it fires once or every time the level is crossed.

Step 3 — Turn on phone notifications

To get alerts on your phone when the site isn't open, sign in and switch on phone notifications. RealChart then watches your alerts in the background and sends a push notification to your device when one triggers — no app to install.

On iPhone, add RealChart to your Home Screen first (Share → Add to Home Screen), then open it from there and enable notifications — that's an Apple requirement for web push.

Tips for better alerts

  • Set alerts slightly ahead of the exact level so you have time to react.
  • Use "once" for one-off targets and "every time" for levels you trade repeatedly.
  • Combine an alert with a drawn trendline or horizontal level so you know exactly why it fired.
